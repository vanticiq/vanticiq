# VanticLab

We build software that makes AI work reliably for 
the businesses that can't afford for it not to.

Most companies didn't plan their AI deployment —
they accumulated it. The result is AI that makes 
commitments it shouldn't, speaks in a voice that 
isn't theirs, and handles sensitive situations 
without guardrails.

We fix that structurally, not with better prompts.

---

**Vantic IQ** maps how work actually moves through 
a business — where time disappears, where handoffs 
fail, where friction compounds. No configuration. 
No content access. No surveillance.

Every boundary in our system is a structural 
constraint. Enforced at data ingestion. Not 
declared in a document.
